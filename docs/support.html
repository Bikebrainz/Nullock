Common questions

Can I use the free version in commercial work?
Yes. The desktop app is MIT-licensed, no restrictions. Sell pen-tests with it, use it in CI, embed the CLI in your build pipeline -- all fine. Premium is for support, not for permission.
What if I need a non-MIT commercial license for my product?
The Community edition under MIT already covers basically any commercial use -- you keep the LICENSE.md attribution and you're done. If your legal team specifically needs a non-MIT license for indemnity reasons, email us (Enterprise tier).
Do I get priority over GitHub issues?
Premium and Business sponsors get a private email channel. We try to triage your issues within the SLA window before public issues. Critical security reports always jump everything regardless of tier.
What's the difference between Premium and a GitHub Sponsorship?
Sponsorship money funds the project (signing certs, infra, maintainer time). Premium money also gets you a support relationship. If you only want to fund Nullock, sponsor. If you want help, buy Premium.
What does "custom extension review" mean?
You wrote a JS extension and want a second pair of eyes before deploying it across your team -- you send it, we read it, comment back on security / performance / correctness within the SLA window. Capped at one extension per month per Premium customer.
Refund policy?
Email us within 30 days of purchase, no questions, full refund. Beyond 30 days, prorated.
Are you a company?
Right now I'm a single maintainer operating as a sole proprietor. The Premium and Business tiers are designed to be servable by one person. If the business grows, the support model will too.
