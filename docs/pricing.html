Why the prices are low. A single human maintains Nullock today; pricing reflects what one person can sustainably deliver. As the team grows, more tiers may appear -- the existing tiers grandfather for the life of the subscription.
Fair use. "Named seats" means individual people. Sharing a single Premium account across a 50-person team violates the fair-use understanding -- if more than a couple of folks at your company are getting email support, please bump to Business or talk to us about Enterprise.
Refunds. 30-day no-questions refund. After 30 days, prorated. Cancel any time at the bottom of your Stripe receipt email.
