Privacy

Last updated June 2, 2026.

The short version: the desktop app collects nothing. This page covers what the website and paid services touch.

The desktop app

No telemetry, no account, no phone-home. The binary makes no network calls except the traffic you point it at. Your captures live in a local SQLite file and never leave your machine unless you buy Hosted and opt in per scope.

This website

Static pages, no third-party analytics, no ad trackers. Server logs keep IP and user-agent for 14 days to catch abuse, then they're deleted.

Paid services

  • Payments are handled by Stripe. We store your email and a Stripe customer ID — never your card number.
  • Hosted relay stores the captures you choose to sync, encrypted at rest, deleted on the retention schedule for your plan.
  • Support email is kept as long as your subscription is active, then archived for one year for tax reasons.

Your rights

Open a private GitHub Security Advisory to see, export, or delete anything we hold. We reply within 30 days, usually the same week.
